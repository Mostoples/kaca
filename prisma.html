      How to use

      Place an acrylic prism or pyramid with its apex on the centre of the screen and dim the room. Each face reflects one view, and the four meet as a single image that appears to float inside the prism.

      A screen laid flat (a tablet, or a monitor tilted back) works best. Adjust Face size and Distance to centre until the four reflections overlap, and turn on Mirror if the image reads backwards.

      Space play/pause · ← → step one angle · F full screen · P hide panel · drag on the stage to rotate by hand.

      Interface prototype, not a medical device. The volume comes from a synthetic phantom or from files you open yourself, all processed in this browser.
