How it works

From PACS to prism in four steps

  1. 01

    Pull from PACS

    Series are retrieved over DICOMweb straight from the hospital archive.

  2. 02

    Reconstruct on-prem

    An on-premise GPU rebuilds the volume. No rendering server, no data leaving the network.

  3. 03

    Project four views

    A four-view emitter panel feeds a glass prism, so anatomy appears suspended inside it.

  4. 04

    Control touchless

    On-device speech recognition and time-of-flight hand tracking keep the field sterile.
